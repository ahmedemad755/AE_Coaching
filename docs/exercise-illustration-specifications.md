## High-to-Low Cable Fly — تفتيح كابل من أعلى لأسفل

ID: `ex_high_to_low_cable_fly`. Catalog: chest; cable.

Required variation: high pulleys; downward inward path

**Starting position:** Stand between two cable towers with pulleys visibly above shoulder level. Arms open, elbows softly bent, torso steady in a split stance.

**Ending position:** Hands meet in front of the lower chest/upper abdomen after moving downward and inward; retain the same soft elbow angle.

**Joint movement:** Shoulder adduction and horizontal adduction along a downward diagonal; elbows remain approximately fixed.

**Equipment:** Two high adjustable pulleys, two single handles, visible taut cable runs and weight-stack housings.

**Grip and stance:** One handle per hand, wrists aligned with forearms, split stance and soft knees.

**Camera angle:** Front three-quarter view wide enough to show both elevated pulley origins and the lower hand finish.

**Primary muscles to highlight:** pectoralis major

**Visual acceptance requirements:** Show both pulley origins; cable lines alone do not establish pulley height.; End lower than the starting hands; do not depict the upward fly or a chest press.; Keep elbow bend and torso angle consistent across panels.

**Ordered phases:** Stand between two cable towers with pulleys visibly above shoulder level. Arms open, elbows softly bent, torso steady in a split stance. → Hands meet in front of the lower chest/upper abdomen after moving downward and inward; retain the same soft elbow angle.

## Low-to-High Cable Fly — تفتيح كابل من أسفل لأعلى

ID: `ex_low_to_high_cable_fly`. Catalog: chest; cable.

Required variation: low pulleys; upward inward path

**Starting position:** Stand between cable towers with both pulleys visibly near ankle level; handles beside and slightly behind the hips, elbows softly bent.

**Ending position:** Hands meet in front of the upper chest after an upward inward arc, without turning the movement into an overhead press.

**Joint movement:** Shoulder flexion with horizontal adduction on an ascending diagonal; little change in elbow angle.

**Equipment:** Two low adjustable cable pulleys and individual handles; show the low anchor points.

**Grip and stance:** Neutral-to-supinated comfortable handle grip, neutral wrists, balanced staggered stance.

**Camera angle:** Front three-quarter view including floor-level pulleys and the upper-chest finish.

**Primary muscles to highlight:** pectoralis major, clavicular region

**Visual acceptance requirements:** Both cable origins stay low in every panel.; Hands travel upward and inward, not down toward the abdomen.; Use an arm arc with a stable soft elbow bend, not a curl or press.

**Ordered phases:** Stand between cable towers with both pulleys visibly near ankle level; handles beside and slightly behind the hips, elbows softly bent. → Hands meet in front of the upper chest after an upward inward arc, without turning the movement into an overhead press.

## Single-Arm Cable Chest Fly — تفتيح صدر كابل ذراع واحدة

ID: `ex_single_arm_cable_chest_fly`. Catalog: chest; cable.

Required variation: one arm; chest-height cable; no pressing

**Starting position:** Stand side-on to one chest-height pulley. Working arm opens to the side with a soft elbow bend; nonworking hand rests on the hip.

**Ending position:** Working hand sweeps inward to the front of the opposite side of the chest while the torso remains facing forward.

**Joint movement:** Unilateral shoulder horizontal adduction with a nearly constant elbow angle; trunk resists rotation.

**Equipment:** One adjustable cable tower, pulley at chest height and one D-handle.

**Grip and stance:** Single handle with neutral wrist; stable split stance, feet remain planted.

**Camera angle:** Front three-quarter view from the working side, showing the chest-height pulley and full arm arc.

**Primary muscles to highlight:** pectoralis major

**Visual acceptance requirements:** Only one arm holds a cable handle.; No dumbbell, second cable or bench.; Do not bend and extend the elbow like a unilateral chest press or rotate the whole torso.

**Ordered phases:** Stand side-on to one chest-height pulley. Working arm opens to the side with a soft elbow bend; nonworking hand rests on the hip. → Working hand sweeps inward to the front of the opposite side of the chest while the torso remains facing forward.

## Reverse Lunge — لانجز خلفي

ID: `ex_reverse_lunge`. Catalog: quadriceps; bodyweight.

Required variation: bodyweight; step backward

**Starting position:** Stand tall with feet hip width, empty hands at the hips or sides and no external load.

**Ending position:** Step one foot backward and lower into a split stance with the front foot flat, front knee bent and rear knee near the floor; then return to the original stance.

**Joint movement:** Hip and knee flexion during the backward step and descent, followed by front-leg hip/knee extension on return.

**Equipment:** Bodyweight only on a level floor; no dumbbells, barbell, Smith bar or kettlebell.

**Grip and stance:** Hands empty; feet maintain hip-width separation rather than crossing behind each other.

**Camera angle:** Side three-quarter view with a small direction arrow showing the stepping foot traveling backward.

**Primary muscles to highlight:** quadriceps; gluteus maximus

**Visual acceptance requirements:** No external load.; Sequence must establish a backward step, not a forward/walking lunge.; Front heel stays planted and knee tracks with the foot; no crossed curtsy stance.

**Ordered phases:** Upright start, both feet level. → One foot steps backward into a controlled lunge. → Return the same rear foot forward to the starting stance.

Required variation: bodyweight; heels elevated; narrow stance; not goblet
